TikTok Integration

Your own accounts. Your choices on every post. Always initiated by you.

WonderCut integrates with TikTok through the official TikTok for Developers platform, using Login Kit to connect an account and the Content Posting API's Direct Post flow to publish videos. The integration has one purpose: publishing finished videos from WonderCut on your computer to your own TikTok account, with every posting choice made by you on WonderCut's TikTok posting screen.

How it works

1

In WonderCut's settings, you connect your TikTok account through TikTok's own login and consent page, opened in your browser. WonderCut never sees your password.

2

After reviewing a batch, you choose a finished video and open "Post to TikTok". WonderCut asks TikTok for your account's current posting settings each time, and shows which account (by its TikTok nickname) the video will be posted to.

3

On the posting screen you see a preview of the video, edit its caption and hashtags, and choose who can view it from the options TikTok allows for your account. Nothing is pre-selected: you pick the audience every time.

4

You decide whether others can comment, duet, or stitch. These start switched off; any your TikTok account already disables are shown greyed out.

5

If the video promotes your own brand or a third party, you disclose it. WonderCut shows the label TikTok will apply ("Promotional content" or "Paid partnership"), and branded content can't be set to private.

6

You confirm by pressing Post, under TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation (and Branded Content Policy, when it applies). Only then is the video uploaded. WonderCut shows its progress and tells you that TikTok may take a few minutes to process it before it appears on your profile.

Permissions used

user.info.basic
Reads the connected account's display name and avatar, used only to label the account inside the app.
user.info.profile
Reads the account's username (handle), so you can tell your connected accounts apart.
video.publish
Publishes a video to the connected account, only when you press Post on the posting screen, with the audience, interaction settings, and disclosure you chose there. WonderCut also reads your account's posting settings (allowed audiences, interaction settings, and maximum video length) to build that screen.

What the integration never does

It never posts without you pressing Post, never chooses an audience for you, never adds watermarks or promotional branding to your videos, never touches accounts you haven't connected, and never reads followers, analytics, or messages. Access tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage. Disconnecting an account in settings revokes its token with TikTok.

WonderCut is independently developed. Private alpha; public beta planned for 2027.
