TikTok Integration

Drafts only. The band's own accounts only. Always user-initiated.

WonderCut integrates with TikTok through the official TikTok for Developers platform, using Login Kit for account authorization and the Content Posting API's upload flow for delivery. The integration exists for one purpose: moving finished videos from the desktop editing pipeline to the phone, where they are reviewed and posted by hand.

How it works

1

In WonderCut's settings, a band account is connected once through TikTok's own login and consent page, opened in the system browser. The app never sees the account password.

2

On the Mass Export screen, the user picks a connected account and sends an exported video, or a whole approved batch, to it.

3

Each video is uploaded to TikTok as an inbox draft. A notification appears in that account's TikTok app inbox: "Your content from WonderCut is ready."

4

Tapping the notification opens TikTok's own editor, where the video is captioned, finished, and posted manually, or discarded.

Permissions used

user.info.basic
Reads the connected account's display name and avatar, used only to label the account inside the app.
user.info.profile
Reads the account's username (the handle), shown so the right account is always chosen when several are connected.
video.upload
Uploads videos to the connected account's inbox as drafts. The app holds no permission to publish content and never posts publicly.

What the integration never does

It never posts publicly or on a schedule, never touches accounts other than the band's own connected accounts, never reads followers, analytics, or messages, and never acts without an explicit click. Access tokens are stored only on the band's own computer, encrypted with the operating system's secure storage. Disconnecting an account in settings revokes its token with TikTok.
