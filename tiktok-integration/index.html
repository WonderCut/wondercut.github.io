TikTok Integration

Drafts only. Your own accounts only. Always initiated by you.

WonderCut integrates with TikTok through the official TikTok for Developers platform, using Login Kit to connect an account and the Content Posting API's upload flow to deliver videos. The integration has one purpose: moving finished videos from WonderCut on your computer to your TikTok app, where you review, caption, and post them yourself.

How it works

1

In WonderCut's settings, you connect your TikTok account through TikTok's own login and consent page, opened in your browser. WonderCut never sees your password.

2

After reviewing a batch, you pick a connected account and send an exported video, or a set of approved videos, to it.

3

Each video is uploaded to TikTok as a draft, and a notification appears in that account's TikTok inbox: "Your content from WonderCut is ready."

4

Tapping the notification opens TikTok's editor, where you finish and post the video, or discard it.

Permissions used

user.info.basic
Reads the connected account's display name and avatar, used only to label the account inside the app.
user.info.profile
Reads the account's username (handle), so you can tell your connected accounts apart.
video.upload
Uploads videos to the connected account as drafts. WonderCut holds no permission to publish and never posts publicly.

What the integration never does

It never posts publicly, never touches accounts you haven't connected, never reads followers, analytics, or messages, and never sends anything without your explicit action. Access tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage. Disconnecting an account in settings revokes its token with TikTok.

WonderCut is independently developed. Private alpha; public beta planned for 2027.
