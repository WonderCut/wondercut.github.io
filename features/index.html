Features

Everything between a raw take and a finished vertical video.

Word-accurate lyric sync

Each song's lyrics are force-aligned to its audio, so every word carries a start and end time. Sections (verse, chorus, bridge) are marked on the waveform, and each section can carry multiple tapped beat progressions that drive cutting. Sync can be refined by hand in a dedicated editor when the automatic alignment needs correction.

Multi-cam sync and beat cutting

Multiple camera angles of one performance are aligned against a parent take to form a multi-cam group. Cuts between angles are generated from beats tapped in time with the music, with controls for avoiding repeated angles, using saved crop variations, and falling back to the parent angle where coverage runs out. Every segment stays editable before export.

Caption and font templates

Caption styling is template-driven: font family and weight, fine typography (tracking, line height, per-word emphasis), text and background colors, animation mode, and placement are saved as reusable templates that apply across songs. Hooks, the attention-grabbing opening text, have their own layout system with a drag-to-position preview.

Footage library

All clips across all songs live in one tagged library: song tags, folder tags, and custom tags combine into queries that drive automatic clip selection. B-Roll clips carry marked highlight ranges so only their best moments are used. Per-clip framings define how each source clip is cropped for vertical and 4:3 output, and framings can themselves be tagged and filtered.

Intro clips

An edit can open on a chosen clip with its own audio at full volume while the song ducks underneath and fades up as the edit begins. Clips are auditioned in a scrubbing preview where the exact segment to use is marked in and out before it is placed.

Mass export pipeline

Styles define recipes: a multi-cam, or a song plus a tag query. The generator rolls batches of randomized draft videos from those recipes, assigning hooks from curated pools. Drafts are previewed inline, re-rolled, edited, and approved or rejected. Approved items render to MP4 in batch, with failures reported per item and per style.

Delivery to TikTok

At the end of the pipeline, approved and rendered videos are sent to the band's own TikTok accounts as inbox drafts using TikTok's official Content Posting API. Posting always happens manually from the TikTok app. See TikTok Integration for how this works and which permissions it uses.
