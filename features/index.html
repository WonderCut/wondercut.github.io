Features

Everything between a raw take and a finished vertical video.

Word-accurate lyric sync

Each song's lyrics are aligned to its audio, so every word carries a start and end time. Sections (verse, chorus, bridge) are marked on the waveform, and each section can carry tapped beat progressions that drive cutting. Timing can be refined by hand when the automatic alignment needs correction.

Multi-cam sync and beat cutting

Multiple camera angles of one performance are aligned into a multi-cam group. Cuts between angles are generated from beats tapped in time with the music, with controls for avoiding repeated angles and using saved crop variations. Every cut stays editable before export.

Caption and font templates

Caption styling is template-driven: font, weight, tracking, line height, colors, backgrounds, animation (including word-by-word reveals), and placement are saved as reusable templates that apply across songs. Hooks, the attention-grabbing opening text, have their own layout and can be drafted with AI assistance.

Footage library

All clips across all songs live in one tagged library: song, folder, and custom tags combine into queries that drive automatic clip selection. B-roll clips carry highlight ranges so only their best moments are used, and per-clip framings define how each clip is cropped for vertical and 4:3 output.

Mass export pipeline

Styles are recipes: a multi-cam, or a song plus a tag query. The generator rolls batches of draft videos from those recipes and assigns hooks. Drafts are previewed, re-rolled, edited, and approved or rejected; approved items render to MP4 in batch.

Live shows

Full-length concert recordings are split into per-song highlights that become multi-cam groups, so a single show can feed weeks of content.

Companion iPhone app (in development)

Browse generated batches, preview, approve, and make quick edits from your phone, connected privately to WonderCut on your Mac.

Delivery to TikTok

Approved, rendered videos are sent to your own connected TikTok accounts as drafts using TikTok's official Content Posting API. You finish and post them from the TikTok app. See TikTok Integration for how this works and which permissions it uses.

WonderCut is independently developed. Private alpha; public beta planned for 2027.
