About

The band

AKILA & The Wonder Machine is the musical project of Akila Kavisinghe. The band records original songs and performs live, and shares its music primarily through short-form vertical video: lyric clips, live cuts, and behind-the-scenes footage.

Why WonderCut exists

Publishing music content consistently means producing many videos from the same pool of songs and footage. Doing that by hand in a general-purpose editor does not scale for one person. WonderCut encodes the band's whole workflow, from lyric syncing to batch rendering to delivery, so producing a week of content takes an evening instead of a week.

Who runs it

WonderCut and this website are built, operated, and used by Akila Kavisinghe. The app is not distributed to or offered for use by the general public, and it connects only to the band's own TikTok accounts.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download WonderCut?
No. It is a personal tool and is not distributed.
Does WonderCut post to TikTok automatically?
No. It delivers drafts to the band's own account inboxes; every post is reviewed and published manually from the TikTok app.
Does WonderCut collect any data?
No. It runs entirely on the band's own computer, operates no servers, and stores TikTok tokens locally, encrypted. See the Privacy Policy.
