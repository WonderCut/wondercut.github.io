About

Why WonderCut exists

Independent musicians are expected to publish short-form video every day, usually from the same handful of songs and performances. Doing that by hand in a general-purpose video editor takes far more time than most artists have. WonderCut turns that whole workflow, from lyric syncing to batch rendering to delivery, into something a single person can do in an evening.

Who makes it

WonderCut is designed and built by an independent developer who is also a working musician. It began as a tool for the developer's own releases and is now being prepared for other artists.

Where it is now

Private alpha (now)
WonderCut is in daily use in its developer's own release workflow while its features and reliability mature.
Beta (planned for 2027)
Invited musicians and creators connect their own accounts and use WonderCut on their own music. Request beta access.
Public release
After the beta, based on what testers need.

Frequently asked questions

Can I try WonderCut?
Not yet publicly. Beta invitations open in 2027. Request beta access and you'll be contacted when they do.
How do I get in touch?
Use the contact link.
What does it run on?
macOS. A companion iPhone app is in development.
Does WonderCut post to TikTok automatically?
No. Nothing is posted until you press Post on the TikTok posting screen, where you preview the video and choose its caption, audience, interaction settings, and disclosure every time.
Where is my data stored?
On your own computer. See the Privacy Policy for the few services WonderCut uses and what they receive.
