WonderCut

The desktop video studio behind AKILA & The Wonder Machine's short-form music content.

WonderCut is a purpose-built desktop application (macOS) that Akila Kavisinghe uses to turn the band's live footage, studio takes, and music into finished vertical videos. It handles the whole workflow: syncing lyrics to audio word by word, cutting between camera angles on tapped beats, styling captions, batch-generating dozens of draft videos, and delivering the approved ones to the band's own TikTok accounts as drafts.

It is a personal production tool, not a commercial product. It is not distributed to or offered for use by the general public.

What it does

Lyric sync

Aligns every word of a song's lyrics to the audio, producing word-accurate timing that drives all caption rendering.

Multi-cam cutting

Syncs different camera angles of the same take and cuts between them on beats tapped in time with the song.

Caption design

Reusable caption and font templates: typography, colors, backgrounds, animation, and per-section overrides.

Mass export

Batch-generates randomized draft videos from saved styles, with an approve and reject curation flow before anything renders.

Footage library

A tagged, cross-song clip library with highlight marking, framing presets, and per-clip crop variations.

TikTok drafts

Approved exports go to the band's own TikTok inboxes as drafts, to be reviewed and posted from the phone.

How a video gets made

1

A song's audio and lyrics are imported and synced, giving every word a timestamp.

2

Footage is organized: multi-cam takes are aligned to the song, B-Roll is tagged and highlighted, framings are saved for vertical output.

3

The mass exporter rolls batches of candidate videos from saved styles; each draft is previewed, tweaked, and approved or discarded.

4

Approved drafts render to MP4 through a Remotion-based render pipeline.

5

Finished videos are sent to the band's own TikTok accounts as inbox drafts through TikTok's official Content Posting API, then reviewed and posted by hand. Details here.
