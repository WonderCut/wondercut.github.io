WonderCut

The short-form video studio for musicians. Turn your songs and footage into batches of finished, captioned vertical videos.

WonderCut is in private alpha. A beta for invited musicians and creators is planned for 2027.

Musicians are expected to post short-form video constantly, but cutting dozens of lyric clips, live cuts, and performance edits by hand in a general-purpose editor doesn't scale. WonderCut is a macOS app built for exactly that job: it syncs your lyrics to your songs word by word, cuts between your camera angles on the beat, styles the captions, generates batches of draft videos for you to review, and delivers the approved ones to your social accounts.

What it does

Lyric sync

Aligns every word of a song's lyrics to the audio, so captions land exactly when each word is sung.

Multi-cam cutting

Syncs camera angles of the same performance and cuts between them on beats you tap in time with the song.

Caption design

Reusable caption and font templates: typography, colors, backgrounds, animation, and hook text.

Mass export

Generates batches of draft videos from saved styles, with an approve-and-reject review before anything renders.

Footage library

A tagged clip library with highlight marking, framing presets, and per-clip crops for vertical output.

Delivery

Approved videos go to your own TikTok accounts as drafts, ready to finish and post from your phone.

How a video gets made

1

Import a song's audio and lyrics; WonderCut syncs them, giving every word a timestamp.

2

Add your footage: multi-cam takes are aligned to the song, B-roll is tagged and highlighted, framings are saved for vertical output.

3

The mass exporter rolls batches of candidate videos from your saved styles; you preview, tweak, and approve or discard each one.

4

Approved videos render to MP4.

5

Finished videos are sent to your connected TikTok accounts as drafts through TikTok's official Content Posting API, and you post them from the TikTok app. How the TikTok integration works.
