Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

WonderCut ("the App") is a personal desktop application built and operated by Akila Kavisinghe for the band AKILA & The Wonder Machine. It runs entirely on the user's own computer and is not distributed to or offered for use by the general public. This policy describes what data the App handles and how.

1. Data the App handles

All video, audio, and project files you work with in the App stay on your computer. The App operates no servers and collects no analytics, telemetry, or usage data.

2. TikTok data

When you connect a TikTok account, the App receives through TikTok's official API, with your authorization: your account's open ID, display name, username, and avatar (scopes user.info.basic and user.info.profile), and the ability to upload video drafts to your account's inbox (scope video.upload).

This information and the associated access tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage where available. They are never transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's own API. Videos are uploaded to TikTok only when you explicitly initiate an upload, and only as drafts to your own account.

3. Sharing

The App does not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party. The only external service the TikTok integration communicates with is TikTok itself.

4. Data deletion

Disconnecting a TikTok account in the App's settings revokes its access token with TikTok and deletes the stored tokens and profile information from your computer. You can also revoke the App's access from your TikTok account's security settings at any time.
