Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

WonderCut ("the App") is a desktop application for creating short-form music videos, developed independently. It is currently in private alpha, with a beta for invited users planned. This policy describes what data the App handles, where it goes, and how to delete it.

1. Your projects stay on your computer

Your songs, footage, projects, and rendered videos are stored on your own computer. The App does not collect analytics, telemetry, or usage data.

2. Services the App sends data to

Some features send specific content to a service provider, only when you use that feature:

TikTok
When you connect an account and send a video. See section 3.
AudioShake (lyric alignment)
A song's audio and lyrics, to compute word timings.
Anthropic (AI text suggestions)
Song titles, lyrics, hook text, and your notes, to draft hooks and post descriptions.
Google Gemini (AI song analysis)
A song's audio and your notes, to suggest how a song or performance could be split up.
Cloudflare (optional post queue)
Videos you choose to queue for posting from your phone, with their post text. Queued media is deleted when you mark the post as posted, or when you remove it from the queue.

Each provider processes this content under its own terms and privacy policy. The App does not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

3. TikTok data

When you connect a TikTok account, the App receives through TikTok's official API, with your authorization: your account's open ID, display name, username, and avatar (scopes user.info.basic and user.info.profile), and the ability to upload video drafts to your account (scope video.upload).

This information and the access tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage where available, and are sent only to TikTok's own API. Videos are uploaded only when you explicitly send them, and only as drafts to your own account.

4. Companion iPhone app

The companion app connects directly to WonderCut on your own Mac, over your local network or a private network you set up. Videos it downloads for previewing are stored on your phone and can be removed at any time from its storage settings.

5. Deleting your data

Disconnecting a TikTok account in the App's settings revokes its access token with TikTok and deletes the stored tokens and profile information from your computer. You can also revoke access from your TikTok account's security settings. Your project files are yours to delete like any other files on your computer. To have any data held in the post queue deleted, remove it in the App.

WonderCut is independently developed. Private alpha; public beta planned for 2027.
